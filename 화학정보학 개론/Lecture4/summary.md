Drugs are an ill-defined entity from a chemical standpoint.

Drug like compound: 우리 몸에 흡수되어서 부작용이 없어야 한다. 
--> ADMET 성질을 갖는 compound

1. Lipinski's Rule of five
The rule of five states that poor absorption or permeability are more likely when.... 
-cLogP is > 5 그럼 나쁘다.
(the calculated 1-octanol-water partition coefficient, a measure of lipophilicity)
-molecular mass is > 500Da 너무 커서 나쁘다.
-the number of hydrogen-bond donors is > 5 그럼 나쁘다
-the number of hydrogen-bond acceptors is > 10 그럼 나쁘다

--> leading measure of durg likeness

2. Pharmacodynamics(what the drug does to biological system)
  -> Toxicity
3. Pharmacokinetics(what the biological system does to the drug)
   -> ADME

-Lipophilicity
이게 별로 변하지 않는다는 건 drug likeness이다.
왜냐하면 이건 drug potency, toxicity등에 영향을 줌
만약 너무 높으면 다양한 타겟에 붙을 가능성이 높아져서 위험해진다.
